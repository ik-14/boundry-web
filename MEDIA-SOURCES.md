## Current film: AI in motion (v3)

The current film is an original procedural animation created for Boundry in HyperFrames. Its animated imagery is generated within the project; it uses no stock footage or copied reference footage. The [Kimi K3 HyperFrames remake](https://static.heygen.ai/hyperframes-oss/desktop/viral-clone/remix-kimi-k3-result.mp4), featured in the [HyperFrames examples gallery](https://hyperframes.heygen.com/examples), served only as a motion and style reference.

The narrative moves from physical and digital AI, through new technology and risk, to understanding those risks and making the case for cover. It closes with “Insurance for what comes next.” The imagery is conceptual and does not depict customers, endorsements or completed insurance outcomes.

The editable project is kept in the adjacent `boundry-ai-in-motion-film` workspace directory. Website exports are `boundry-ai-in-motion.mp4`, `boundry-ai-in-motion.webm` and `boundry-ai-in-motion-poster.webp`.
